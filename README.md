# de-id-public

Public releases of the De-ID tool. Website: https://hantswilliams.github.io/de-id-public/

## macOS: 0.5.1 (beta, recommended)

- [Release notes](https://github.com/hantswilliams/de-id-public/releases/tag/desktop-v0.5.1)
- macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon (M1 or newer), signed and notarized:
  [De-ID_0.5.1_aarch64.dmg](https://github.com/hantswilliams/de-id-public/releases/download/desktop-v0.5.1/De-ID_0.5.1_aarch64.dmg)

## Windows: 0.4.3 (beta, earlier version)

- [Release notes](https://github.com/hantswilliams/de-id-public/releases/tag/desktop-v0.4.3)
- Windows 10 or 11, x64 (Windows 11 on ARM runs it under emulation):
  [De-ID_0.4.3_x64-setup.exe](https://github.com/hantswilliams/de-id-public/releases/download/desktop-v0.4.3/De-ID_0.4.3_x64-setup.exe).
  It doesn't have what's new in 0.5 (the clipboard round trip, the AI terminal,
  the De-ID folder, Finder and the menu bar). The installer isn't signed yet: when
  SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
